## Findings

### BC-01 — The Find anything box on Home showed no focus
- **Who it blocks:** Someone using a keyboard could not see when the Find anything box on Home had focus, so typing seemed to go nowhere. (keyboard, low vision)
- **Fix:** Give the field its own 2px accent outline when focused (form.ask input:focus-visible).
- **Severity and effort:** Major · S (0.25–1 h) · root cause: focus-ring
- **For your developer:** atlas/assets/hub/hub.css:268 — 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA) — found by rendering (render: focus-not-visible)
- **Status:** Fixed and verified — render-check.mjs: focus-not-visible for "Find anything" no longer reported on /hub (re-run 2026-10-23)

### BC-02 — Appearance switches were 40x22 and crowded
- **Who it blocks:** The High contrast and Reduce motion switches in the Aa menu were under 24 px tall and close together, so a shaky hand or finger could hit the wrong one. (motor, touch)
- **Fix:** Extend each switch link 4 px beyond the drawn pill (inset:-4px).
- **Severity and effort:** Minor · S (0.25–1 h) · root cause: toggle-target
- **For your developer:** atlas/assets/hub/hub.css:634 — 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) (Level AA) — found by rendering (render: target-size)
- **Status:** Fixed and verified — render-check.mjs: target-size for "contrast on" no longer reported on /hub/talk (re-run 2026-10-23)

## Before you ship — 5-minute check
No screen reader was used by this audit. Use NVDA with Firefox on Windows, or VoiceOver with Safari on a Mac (Cmd+F5). You do not need to know code.
1. Turn on NVDA with Firefox on Windows, or VoiceOver with Safari on a Mac (Cmd+F5), open the main screen and move through it one item at a time (Tab key on a computer, swipe right on a phone). Every button should say what it does.
2. Complete the main task once using only the keyboard (or only the screen reader). Note anywhere you get stuck or hear something unclear.
3. Make text larger (browser zoom 200%, or the phone's largest text size) and check nothing is cut off.

## Glossary
- **WCAG 2.2 AA**: the international accessibility standard, at the level most laws and app stores ask for.
- **Focus**: the marker showing which control the keyboard will act on next.
- **Target size**: how big a button or link is to tap or click; 24 px is the minimum.
- **Screen reader**: software that reads the screen aloud for blind and low-vision people.
- **Forced colours**: Windows' high-contrast mode, which replaces a page's colours with the user's own.
- **axe-core**: a widely used automatic accessibility rule checker.
