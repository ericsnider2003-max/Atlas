## 3. money — the retained months

`--keep` used to shift this-month into last-month and DROP what was there, so
"is this normal for me?" was unanswerable while `judgment::ordinary_for` sat
ready. New: `money::MONTHS` (24 kept months, summaries not statements, oldest
out first), written at the same `--keep` that used to destroy the history, and
`money::unusual_buckets` — each spending bucket measured against its own
months, median and MAD through `judgment`, spoken only past 2 spreads and a
6-month floor. A month with nothing in a bucket counts as an observation of
zero, not a missing row. This is the shape `judgment`'s own doc says
`category_jump`'s single fraction should have been: +50% on steady groceries
speaks, +50% on the wobbly one is a Tuesday — held as a test in
`tests/a_month_against_your_own_months.rs` (6 tests).

## 4. handshape — the twenty samples are kept

`Learning::watch` measured every teaching sample against the universal
thresholds, kept a tally, and dropped the measurements. It now retains them,
and `worked_out` turns them into per-feature `Band`s (median + MAD via
`judgment::ordinary_for` — the tree's one definition of "usually") carried on
the `Gesture` and applied in matching: a hand whose "out" ring finger curls at
0.45 fails the universal 0.4 and passes its own band, capped so "out" can
never come to mean half-curled. The shipped defaults carry no bands on
purpose — they were read off recordings, not taught by the hand that will use
them. Calibration survives the store; pre-calibration saves load unchanged.
Seven new tests in `tests/handshape.rs`.

Also new: `handshape::Trail` — the first production source of `Motion`.
Nothing ever built one with real numbers, so every motion-bound gesture
(swipes, the repeated strokes) was evaluated against `Motion::default()` and
could never fire. Trail turns index-tip positions into across/down
(frame-widths per second — an assumption stated at the struct, to be replaced
by a camera measurement) and reversals-per-second. Three tests.

**Still no teach path:** nothing in production constructs `Learning` — that
needs a "watch this, it means X" intent plus a capture loop, which is a
feature, not a wiring fix. `adopt`/`worked_out`/`recognise`/`recognise_moving`
stay on the test-only list, correctly.

## 5. voiceid — measurement without action

The threshold ruling (may it adapt?) is still Eric's, and this session built
the evidence it needs without touching a threshold: accepted-turn scores are
kept (bounded at 60, cleared by forget), and `thresholds_report` says where
the fixed 0.72 sits in the owner's own score distribution — surfaced in
`atlas doctor`'s voice-lock line, where the person deciding will see it.

`voiceid::forget` had no caller, so an enrolled print could not be revoked
from anywhere in production, which for a credential was a gap. It never
claims to have forgotten what is still on disk. Four new tests in
`tests/voice_lock.rs`.
