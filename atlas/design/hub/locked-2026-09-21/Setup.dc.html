Your data stays with you

Atlas runs on this machine. Your files, calendar, mail and voice stay here — it reaches the internet only for the things you ask that need it. When you share a document or a message with someone, it goes straight to their device, and Atlas shows you before anything leaves.

A note, so it's said plainly: Atlas is provided as-is, with no warranty. You're running it on your own machine, at your own risk — it does a lot on your behalf, and you stay in control of what it's allowed to do.
