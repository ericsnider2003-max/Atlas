Atlas Personal AI Assistant

A personal assistant that lives on your own computer and phone.

Atlas is an assistant you talk or type to. It keeps your reminders, notes and calendar, reads your mail when you ask, answers questions, looks things up, drafts replies and posts for you to approve, and keeps track of what you're working on.

Yours, on your machine

  • No Atlas account and no Atlas server. Atlas runs on your Windows computer, with apps for iPhone and Android that talk to it directly.
  • Its own language model runs on your computer. Your questions are answered there first.
  • What it keeps stays with you, in its own folder on your computer and phone. Passwords and sign-ins are kept in an encrypted vault that opens with your Windows sign-in.

Connecting your accounts

You connect accounts with one button, on each service's own sign-in page — Atlas never sees your password for them:

  • Google, to read your Google Calendar and your own YouTube channel's numbers.
  • Microsoft, for Outlook mail and calendar.
  • Other mail accounts, Bluesky, and the social sites you choose.

What Atlas reads from Google is used only to show you your own calendar and your own channel's numbers, on your own devices. It is never sent to the developer, never sold, never used for advertising, and never sent to an online AI service. See the privacy policy.
